Professional Biography

My background is in Electrical Engineering, and I have gained practical experience in technical work, including electronics and device troubleshooting and repair. These experiences have helped me become more patient, detail-oriented, and comfortable working through problems step by step.

I’m now studying Machine Learning and Artificial Intelligence and developing my interest in Data Analytics. I want to understand not only how new technologies work, but also how they can be useful in real situations. I’m especially interested in the connection between engineering, data, and smarter ways of solving problems.

This portfolio brings together my learning, projects, and reflections. As I progress through my course, I plan to keep adding examples of my work and the lessons I take from each experience.

Personal Value Proposition

What I bring is a combination of engineering knowledge, hands-on technical experience, and a willingness to learn new tools. My engineering background has taught me to look carefully at how systems work, find the cause of a problem, and work toward practical solutions. As I build my skills in AI, Machine Learning, and Data Analytics, I want to apply that same approach to understanding data, improving processes, and making informed decisions.

My goal is to connect traditional engineering problem-solving with the opportunities created by modern data technologies.

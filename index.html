WORKSHOP 1 · ARTIFACT 1

Beyond the Chatbox — AI and ML Timelines

An interactive timeline exploring major and incremental developments in artificial intelligence after the launch of ChatGPT, from November 2022 through October 2026.

Introduction

For our Machine Learning Fundamentals workshop, my group created Beyond the Chatbox to turn our classroom discussion about AI developments into an interactive learning experience.

Description

The website presents selected milestones, explains why each development matters, and distinguishes major breakthroughs from smaller changes in everyday AI use. Visitors can explore events, filter by year, use a guided presentation, and answer a short quiz.

Objective

Our goal was to communicate the evolution of AI clearly to classmates and other learners, while encouraging readers to look beyond headlines and consider how changes in capability, access, and responsible use affect people.

Process

We discussed important AI developments in class, selected events for the timeline, organized the material into major and incremental milestones, drafted explanations, and built an interactive presentation. We explained how generative AI assisted the work.

Tools and Technologies Used

ChatGPT for research organization, event summaries, presentation notes, and website code; an image-generation tool for conceptual cover artwork; HTML, CSS, and JavaScript for the interactive site.

Value Proposition — Unique Value

The timeline brings historical context, event explanations, a guided presentation, and a quiz together in one experience rather than offering a static list of dates.

Value Proposition — Relevance

This project helps me practice communicating technical change to a broad audience. My engineering background encourages me to distinguish what a technology can do from how reliably and responsibly it can be used.

ADDITIONAL COMPLETED COURSEWORK

AI Lab — StudySprint Chatbot and AI Tool Evaluation

In a separate group AI Lab assignment, we developed StudySprint, a conversational study-planning prototype for college students using Mizou, with ChatGPT assistance. We also compared three language-model responses, explored a Study Buddy GPT, and used STORM for a research article.

Project Goal

Help students organize assignments and exam preparation around their deadlines, available hours, breaks, and changing circumstances.

Our Process

We planned the chatbot, configured its instructions and welcome message, tested scenarios, reviewed its responses, and revised its rules. We documented both successes and failures rather than assuming the prototype was reliable.

What Testing Showed

The chatbot asked useful clarifying questions and declined a request to write a graded paper. However, it repeatedly made scheduling and arithmetic errors, including planning work after a deadline. A correct schedule required detailed human feedback.

Tools and Technologies Used

Mizou, ChatGPT, Study Buddy GPT, and STORM. The report documents the scope and limitations of each activity.

What I Learned

AI tools can support planning and explanation, but confident answers still need verification. Checking each time block, total, and deadline was essential to judging the output.
